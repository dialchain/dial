# DialChain Monetary Policy

In the dialchain network declarations are validated by __service providers__ called __validators__ before publication in the Dial log.

Validators are rewarded for providing their services. The amount of the reward is defined by
the network.

Beside validators, __routers__, __data nodes__ and __gateway__ are further components that provide their services against payments.
All services provided by betwork components are subject to fees.

To achieve the above-mentioned, the Dial network needs a medium of exchange for the services. This implies the introduction of currency or money. For more flexibility according to the dialchain specificity the network will introduce its own currency: __Dial__.

## Dial

The __Dial__ is the currency used as medium of exchange.

## Dial Mining

DialCoin mining is the process by which new DialCoins are entered into circulation. It is performed using computers that solve complex computational math problems.

Service consumers or clients (e.x. PloohKeys) generate limited number of dialcoins using the proof of work mechanism to calculate a hash based of a given difficulty defined by the network. Based on the reputation of the client in the network the difficulty will be adjusted (decreased) accordingly. The generated dialcoins can then be used in a defined period of times to pay services (routers, data nodes, gateway and validators).

The proof of work mechanism is introduced to protect the network against spams.

## Dial Usage

__Dial__ coins generated by the Service consumers or clients can only be used to pay services in the network.

Dialcoins received by service providers for achieving their tasks (e.x. declaration validations by validators) are free of usage and can be traded on decentralized exchanges(DEX).

The Dial tresory has the task to control the generation and the usage of the dialCoins.

## Exchange

Dial can be traded on decentralized (e.x. Uniswap) exchanges.
